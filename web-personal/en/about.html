I'm Raúl Antuña, born and raised in Asturias. After finishing my Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering at the University of Oviedo (2022-2026), I joined Hiberus as an AEM intern and since July 2026 I work full time as an AEM Backend Engineer.

At the same time I'm studying the Master's in Web Engineering, where I go deeper into standards, accessibility and advanced web computing. I like well-crafted software: readable code, accessible interfaces and solutions that keep working as time goes by.

Formula 1 team radios

Some of the best radio messages Formula 1 has ever produced. Every audio clip includes a transcript.

  • "GP2 Engine, GP2... Aaargh!" — Fernando Alonso, Japan 2015

    Fernando Alonso's iconic radio complaint at Suzuka, venting his frustration with the engine on the straight as his rivals overtook him.

    Audio transcript

    Fernando Alonso: "GP2 engine! GP2! Aaargh!"

    Alonso compares the Honda engine in his McLaren with those of the junior GP2 series while being overtaken on the Suzuka straight.

  • "Leave me alone, I know what to do" — Kimi Räikkönen, Abu Dhabi 2012

    Kimi Räikkönen leading the race for Lotus at the 2012 Abu Dhabi Grand Prix, giving his engineer one of the most legendary replies in Formula 1 history.

    Audio transcript

    Engineer: "Kimi, keep working on the tyres, we'll keep you updated on the gaps." Kimi Räikkönen: "Just leave me alone, I know what I'm doing." Later: "Yes, yes, yes, I'm doing all the time. You don't have to remind me every second."
